    How your scores were calculated
    Each score is the average of its components. A component measures how far a metric moved from your own resting baseline toward stress/fatigue, capped at a full-scale move (shown per row). A capped bar (100) means that signal was already at or beyond full scale. Heuristic estimate — not a medical measurement.
