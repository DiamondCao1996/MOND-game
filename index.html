02 / PULSE & HRV

Understand the rhythm, gently.

HRV describes the variation in time between heartbeats. MOND is exploring optical pulse sensing (PPG): light helps detect changes in blood volume at the skin, from which pulse timing can be estimated.

Pulse-derived variability is not identical to ECG-measured HRV. Reliable estimates depend on good contact, low motion and signal quality. We’re working toward personal trends, with validation against reference measurements still ahead.

A signal is one part of your story, not a diagnosis or a direct measure of your emotions. MOND is a wellbeing concept in development.

04 / Industry and user research

✧

User research
in virtual reality.

Designed with industry in mind from the beginning. MOND is exploring how physiological signals can complement task performance and participant feedback when teams evaluate a VR experience.

Concept illustration of a proposed workflow, not a completed VR experiment.

  1. 01

    Start with the person

    Agree on consent, record a resting baseline and check sensor contact and signal quality.

  2. 02

    Compare two designs

    Try versions A and B of the same VR task. Change one feature at a time and alternate the order.

  3. 03

    Review the whole experience

    Read HRV alongside errors, completion time and participant feedback. Use the findings to choose the next design change to test.

A few things you might wonder

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Can I buy the wearable today?

MOND is in development. We’re exploring the product concept, technical validation and potential pilot collaborations. Get in touch if you’d like to be part of that conversation.

Who is MOND being designed for?

Our initial focus is workplace wellbeing: helping employees and organisations have more informed conversations about support. The experience and service model are still being developed.

How are you thinking about personal data?

Personal physiological information deserves care. Clear consent, understandable explanations and appropriate access boundaries are design priorities. Pilot data practices will need to be agreed before any collection begins.

How does the game fit in?

The game expresses MOND’s creative world: gentle exploration, curiosity and learning. It is a separate browser experience from the wearable concept, and you can enjoy it right now.
